## 🧩 About

**FragPunk Trainer** is a comprehensive overlay menu for FragPunk featuring recoil control, movement tuning, utility toggles, FOV adjustment, and a practice mode for the Lancers tactical shooter.

Based on popular overlay menus like **OpenHack**, **Eclipse Menu**, and **QOLMod**.

---

## ✨ Features

### 🎮 Core Trainer
- Recoil Control — smooth weapon kick during sustained fire
- Movement Tuner — adjust strafe, slide, and jump timing
- FOV Adjustment — widen or narrow the camera field of view
- Practice Mode — isolate aim drills against static targets
- Overlay Menu — quick toggles without leaving the match

### 🎨 Visuals & Readability
- Hit Marker Overlay — highlight confirmed hits
- Enemy Outline — improve target tracking with edge highlights
- Muzzle Flash Reduction — clearer sight picture during firefights
- Custom Crosshair — pick crosshair styles and colors

### 🏋️ Practice & Training
- Instant Warmup — skip menus into a training lobby
- Recoil Pattern Viewer — study spray patterns per weapon
- Grenade Arc Guide — preview utility trajectories
- Replay Markers — drop timestamps for review
